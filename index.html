JesusAnswers Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

JesusAnswers keeps what you share on your own phone. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not receive, sell or share your questions, prayers, name or phone number. The only thing that reaches us is feedback you choose to send.

What the app keeps on your phone

The following is saved only in the app's storage on your device:

  • Your name, and your mobile number if you choose to enter one. Both are optional and are only used to greet you. They are not verified or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings: language, voice, text size, reading speed and Daily Word reminder time.
  • Your journey: the questions you asked, the Scripture, encouragement and prayers you received, and the entries you saved as favourites.

You can delete a journey entry at any time by swiping it away. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, removes everything it stored.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app uses the microphone only while you are talking to it, after you tap the microphone button. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition service (for example Google on Android or Apple on iOS). That service may process the audio on its servers, under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. We never receive or store recordings. Only the resulting text is kept, in your journey on your phone.

Reading aloud

Answers are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech voice. The text is not sent to us.

Daily Word reminder

If you turn on the reminder, it is scheduled on your phone as a local notification. No server is involved.

Fonts

The first time the app runs, it downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts. As with any web request, this shares your device's IP address with Google. No personal information is included. See Google Fonts privacy.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the verse or answer is passed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.

Feedback you send

If you open Profile → Send feedback and tap Send, your message is delivered to us through Google Forms, together with the app version, your chosen language and your phone's model and Android or iOS version, so we can fix problems on your kind of phone. It does not include your name, phone number, journey or any account. We use it only to improve the app, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Please don't include private details in your message. Google processes the submission under Google's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in servers.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information.

Children

JesusAnswers is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your wellbeing

JesusAnswers offers Scripture, encouragement and prayer. It is not a substitute for pastoral, medical or professional care. If you are in crisis in India, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24×7), or your local emergency number.

Changes to this policy

If a future version sends information off your phone (for example, to answer questions online), we will update this policy and tell you in the app before that happens.
